# They Sell You the Stash. We Make You Carry It Out.

Some extraction shooters charge up to $250 for a bigger stash, better pockets, and a head start. That is a purchased edge in a genre built on equal risk.

Project Coldwater runs on one rule: **the Zone doesn't take a credit card.** Every slot, every rig, every weapon you hold was pulled out of a raid by a player who walked in with the same odds as everyone else.

## Hero Dossier: HOLLOW

**Name:** Kasia Voronova
**Callsign:** HOLLOW
**Status:** Active. 11 confirmed extractions from Sector 9.

Former pipeline welder at the Varta refinery. She was on shift the night containment failed, and she never left. She was the first survivor to run Sector 9 twice.

**Field Trait — Seam Read.** Hollow reads metal. Holding still near pipe runs, catwalks, or tank walls, she picks up footfalls carried through steel up to 40 metres out. They show as a short-lived vibration ping on her HUD. It gives a direction, not an identity. It does not see through walls, and it cuts out the moment she moves.

**Scars.** A chemical burn runs from her left jaw down to her collarbone. The tissue is stiff enough that she is a few frames slower to ADS on the left shoulder. She lost two fingers on her right hand to frostbite on run 14, so the first mag swap after a hard sprint carries a visible fumble. Her respirator has been patched three times and it wheezes, and anyone close can hear it. None of this resets. The player earned every mark alongside her.

**Camera Doctrine.** Hollow moves in third person, so you read the yard, the rooflines, and the gap between containers. The camera obeys her sightline. If she can't see a target, neither can you, so there is no peeking from behind cover. Raise the weapon and the view snaps into first person through her optic, with her breathing and her scarred hand in frame. Drop ADS and you're back out, reading the ground.

## Field Questions

### Is Project Coldwater pay to win?
No. There is no edition, bundle, or purchase that grants stash space, pockets, gear, or matchmaking priority. Every tactical advantage is earned through extraction runs.

### Is there a premium or deluxe edition?
No. There is one edition at one price, and every player starts with the same stash and the same access.

### How do I expand my stash?
By extracting. Stash expansions are earned through successful runs and progression, never bought.

### Is it first-person or third-person?
Both. You move and scout in third person for situational awareness, and aiming down sights snaps you into first-person optics for precision firefights. The third-person camera is limited to your operator's line of sight, so it cannot be used to see around cover.

### Do injuries and scars carry over between runs?
Yes. Heroes accumulate physical scars, wear, and psychological stakes across runs as part of an episodic 3D narrative. What happens to your operator stays with them.
