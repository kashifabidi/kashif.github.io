# Declassified Incident Report

## The Collapse of Sector 4 and the Perimeter Protocols

**Report ID:** GZ-IR-0404
**Classification:** Declassified, partial. Redactions marked ■■■■.
**Subject:** Containment failure, Sector 4 (Blocks A–C and maintenance levels M1–M3)
**Primary surviving witness:** Vance "Sable" Korhonen, Perimeter Maintenance Lead. See the [Sable Hero Dossier](/heroes/sable-dossier/).

---

## 1. Summary

At 13:48 local on Incident Day 0, the primary coolant loop beneath Block C lost pressure. The air scrubbers in Blocks B and C failed shortly after. The sector alarm sounded nine minutes after the first confirmed sensor reading, because a second reading was requested before evacuation was authorised.

206 staff were logged on the Sector 4 day shift. 41 reached the outer perimeter at Gate 11. At 16:02, Command sealed the inner containment line under Perimeter Protocol 7.

The only staff member with working keys to Gate 11, V. Korhonen, declined extraction and remains at the perimeter. Three personnel badge beacons inside Block C have continued to register movement since the seal.

## 3. Perimeter Maps

*For 3D environment and cinematic teams. Descriptions are written top-down, north at the top. Scale notes are approximate.*

### Map 3.1 — Sector 4 Overview

- **Footprint:** a rough rectangle, about 600 m east–west by 400 m north–south.
- **Block A (Administration)** sits in the north-west corner. Three storeys, concrete, mostly intact. Windows blown out on the east face.
- **Block B (Processing)** runs through the centre. Long, low industrial hall with a sawtooth roof. The roof has partially caved in along its southern third.
- **Block C (Coolant and Primary Loop)** fills the south-east corner. Heavy reinforced structure with twin cooling towers. The eastern tower is cracked from base to top and venting a thin, constant pale-green vapour.
- **Maintenance levels M1–M3** run beneath Blocks B and C. These are the Sector 4 bunkers. M2 is flooded ankle-deep with coolant residue.
- **Wind:** the prevailing wind runs west to east, carrying the plume from the cracked tower out over the eastern fence.

### Map 3.2 — Containment Lines

- **Inner containment line:** a concrete barrier wall about 4 m high, circling Blocks B and C at a distance of about 50 m. Sealed at every crossing point. Warning lamps on top pulse amber every two seconds.
- **Outer perimeter fence:** a double chain-link fence topped with razor wire, 3 m high, surrounding the full sector about 120 m out from the inner line. Sensor masts every 80 m, most dark.
- **Dead ground:** the strip between the inner line and the outer fence. Broken vehicles, abandoned equipment carts and scattered personal belongings from the evacuation. Contamination readings rise sharply closer to the inner wall.

### Map 3.3 — The Gates

- **Gate 9 (west) and Gate 10 (south-west):** heavy sliding gates, locked shut since the power failure. Their control booths are dark.
- **Gate 11 (north-east):** the only working exit. A manually cranked sliding gate, held open about 1.5 m by a steel wedge. Beside it is a lit control booth with a camp cot, stacked respirator filters and a hand-drawn map of Block C taped to the glass. This is Sable's post.
- **Gate 12 (south-east):** collapsed under debris from the cracked cooling tower. Impassable.
- **Relay mast:** on the north fence, about 60 m west of Gate 11. A damaged steel lattice mast with a single red beacon lamp and a battery bank at its base. Intercept Log 4-11-A was recovered here.

### Map 3.4 — Block C Interior (Cinematic Focus)

- **Lower corridor:** a long concrete service corridor with a pipe run along the ceiling. Emergency lighting only, dim red. Coolant residue pooled in low sections.
- **Blast door C-2:** a heavy steel door stuck about 70% open after being forced by hand. Deep scrape marks where the fitters pried it with a bar. This is where Sable's hand tremor and forearm burn come from.
- **Beacon zone:** the three moving badge beacons register in the deep east end of Block C, near the primary pump room. Leave this area unrevealed in all cinematics. Show it only as distant beacon pulses on a handheld tracker.

## 5. Perimeter Protocols

These protocols governed the response. Several were followed to the letter. That's part of the problem.

**Protocol 3 — Confirmation Before Evacuation.** A sector-wide evacuation requires a confirmed contamination reading. Invoked at 14:03, when Command requested a second reading, which delayed the alarm by nine minutes.

**Protocol 5 — Gate Lock on Power Loss.** Perimeter gates default to locked if power is lost. When power dropped at 14:58, Gates 9 and 10 locked shut, leaving Gate 11 as the only exit. Gate 11 stayed usable only because Korhonen had it on manual crank.

**Protocol 7 — Inner Line Seal.** Once evacuation traffic stops, the inner containment line is sealed to stop the spread. Sealed at 16:02, with 165 staff unaccounted for.

**Protocol 9 — Staff Extraction.** All remaining staff must evacuate by lift after the seal. Refused by V. Korhonen at 16:19. No enforcement action recorded.
